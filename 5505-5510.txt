interconnect

each channel carries a stereo 16bit serial pcm audio signal, at a varying sample rate.

5505 has 4 serial output channels:
0 - unused
1 - output to 5510 channel 3
2 - output to 5510 channel 2
3 - output to 5510 channel 1

5510 has 4 serial input/output channels:
0 - output to DAC
1 - input from 5505 channel 3
2 - input from 5505 channel 2
3 - input from 5505 channel 1
