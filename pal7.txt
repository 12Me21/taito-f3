pins 1 and 2 ← fdp.120 are the video sync signal, as output to the monitor

pin 3 ← fdp.118 goes high near the end of scanline 255 (that is like, just after the last visible pixel is output), and low near the end of scanline 4 (total duration of 11 scanlines)
which is when the cpu interrupts happen

pin 19 → fdp.121 goes low at the end of vsync (the end of the last long low pulse), for 232 pixels.  this is generated by the pal and sent back to the fdp.

presumably some of the macrocells are being used to create a delay with a binary counter or something here, counting some number of hsync pulses from the falling edge of the fdp.118 pulse, or whatever.

no idea what fdp.121 is actually used for within the fdp of course.
