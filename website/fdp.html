TC0630 FDP
“F3 Display Processor”

The fdp handles all graphics rendering, except for final color lookup and alpha blending.  For each screen pixel, the fdp outputs two color indexes. these are used as addresses for color ram, and the resulting colors are sent to the fda for blending.  The four ram chips to the left of the fdp are the sprite framebuffer.  The two ram chips above the fdp are accessible to the cpu.  These store the tilemaps, list of sprites to render, text/pixel data, and line effects.

Notes

  • graphics ram A8/A10 are swapped (presumably for routing reasons)
  • pin 180 and 207 connect to cartridge A.89 and C.3, fdp.207, and pal9.11.

PAL D77-07 (undumped)

This PAL is only connected to the FDP.  It takes the vsync signal and something else, and outputs something to the next pin of the FDP.
